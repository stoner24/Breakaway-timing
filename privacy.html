Policy

Breakaway Timing · Last updated May 2026

Breakaway Timing ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Breakaway Timing mobile application and website (the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you use the Breakaway Timing tracker, we collect the following:

  • GPS location data — your device's latitude, longitude, and accuracy, collected periodically during an active tracking session
  • Bib number — entered by you at registration
  • Name — optionally entered by you at registration
  • Device information — basic technical data such as GPS accuracy used to improve location quality

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, or any other personal identifiers beyond what you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Display your position on the live spectator map during your race
  • Allow race directors to monitor rider progress and safety
  • Allow friends and family to follow your progress during an event

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to live race tracking.

Location Data

Location data is the core function of the app. Your GPS position is transmitted to our servers and displayed on a public map for the duration of your tracking session. Your location is visible to anyone with the link to the spectator map while tracking is active.

Tracking only occurs when you explicitly start a session by entering your bib number and tapping "Start Tracking." You can stop sharing your location at any time by tapping "Stop Tracking."

On iOS, the app requests "Always" location permission to enable background GPS updates while your screen is off. This permission is used only during an active race tracking session.

All tracking sessions expire automatically after the duration you select (4, 6, 8, or 12 hours). After expiry, your location is no longer shared and your data is removed from the active map.

Data Storage and Retention

Location data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, hosted in the United States. Active session data is retained for the duration of your tracking session and automatically deleted after expiry. We do not retain historical location data beyond the session.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your location data is visible on the public spectator map during an active session. We use Google Firebase as our data infrastructure — their privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You may stop sharing your location at any time by tapping "Stop Tracking" in the app. Because we do not collect email addresses or account information, we cannot verify identity for deletion requests — however, all session data expires and is deleted automatically within the session window you selected.

If you have questions about your data, contact us at breakawayracetiming@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
