How It Works

Three Steps
to Live

01

Enter Your Bib

Open the tracker on your phone before the race. Enter your bib number, select the event, and optionally add your name so spectators can find you on the map.

02

Hit Start

Tap Start Tracking and allow location access when prompted. Your phone will ping your GPS position every 5 minutes automatically. Keep the screen on or it will continue in the background.

03

You're Live

Your dot appears on the spectator map in real time. Friends and family can watch from anywhere. Tracking runs for up to 24 hours and stops automatically when the session expires.
